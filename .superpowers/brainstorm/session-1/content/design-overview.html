Design Specifications: Integrated 3D Overlay HUD

Detailed visual design layout and resolution adaptability plans.

1. Visual Theme (Cyber-Neon Arc & Threat Colors)

The Cyber-Neon Arc style will dynamically shift its accent and glow properties based on the active threat level:

Threat Level Accent Color Neon Secondary Animations
Low Electric Cyan (#06b6d4) Neon Purple/Pink Slow wave, rotating node mesh
Medium Electric Amber (#f59e0b) Cyber Pink Moderate blink, scanning sweep
High/Critical Intense Red (#ef4444) Hot Rose Fast pulse, critical error overlay

2. Integrated 3D Overlay Mode

  • Orbital HUD (Active state): The 3D Neural Nodes Visualizer acts as the centerpiece. Floating telemetry indicators (Shield, Threat, Entropy) are rendered as rounded glass SVG rings layered directly over the 3D particle Canvas.
  • Tactical Grid (Active state): The 3D Neural Nodes canvas will shrink/shift to a smaller widget in the center or background-dim to 10% opacity, letting the grid components take full prominence.

3. Resolution Adaptability (4K vs. 1080p vs. 720p)

  • 4K UHD (3840x2160): Ultra-high-density Bento grid, spacious layouts, full telemetry readings, larger gauges, and rich 3D node structure with 300+ active nodes.
  • 1080p FHD (1920x1080): Standard grid layout, standard component scaling, and 150 nodes to conserve rendering resources.
  • 720p HD (1280x720): Compact stacked layout. The 3D centerpiece shifts to a background element, widgets go single-column, and text sizes and paddings decrease to prevent overflow.
